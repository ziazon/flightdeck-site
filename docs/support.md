# Support

- **Questions and bugs:** open an issue on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/ziazon/flightdeck-site/issues). Say which build you run, what you
  did, what you expected and what happened instead.
- **The guide:** most answers are in the [user guide](/guide/getting-started).
- **Never paste a token, password or other credential** into an issue. Flightdeck keeps those in
  your Mac's Keychain, and no one helping you needs them.
